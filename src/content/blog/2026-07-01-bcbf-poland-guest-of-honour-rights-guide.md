<p>The Polish Book Institute is taking applications for space on its collective stand at Bologna Children's Book Fair 2027. The deadline is <strong>December 8, 2026</strong>, and applicants must register in the Institute's foreign-fairs portal before submitting the form.</p>

<p>The <a href="https://www.instytutksiazki.pl/aktualnosci/zapraszamy-do-nadsylania-zgloszen-na-targi-ksiazki-dla-dzieci-w-bolonii/">official call</a> lists two before-tax options: <strong>PLN 700 for one shelf</strong> or <strong>PLN 2,500 for one rack</strong>. Places are limited. That makes this a short decision window, but it does not mean every complete application will be accepted.</p>

<h2 class="mt-10 mb-4 text-2xl font-bold text-gray-900">Check eligibility before comparing the prices</h2>

<p>The linked <a href="https://www.instytutksiazki.pl/wp-content/uploads/2026/11/regulamin_mtk.pdf">Polish-language participation rules</a> define the programme more narrowly than the general fair audience. It is for Polish publishers, literary agencies, and organizers of international book fairs that promote Polish literature.</p>

<p>An author, translator, illustrator, or rights consultant may qualify to visit Bologna as a professional, but that does not by itself make the person eligible for this collective-stand service. If the applying organization does not fit the Institute's categories, confirm eligibility with the Institute before spending time on the form.</p>

<p>The rules also say that a correct and complete form is a condition of consideration, not a guarantee. The Institute reserves the right to select applications and may limit the number of shelves or racks requested. Outstanding payments owed to the Institute can also lead to rejection.</p>

<h2 class="mt-10 mb-4 text-2xl font-bold text-gray-900">Shelf or rack is a catalogue decision</h2>

<p>A shelf is roughly 90 centimetres wide. A rack contains at least three and no more than four shelves of about the same width, plus storage whose dimensions will be supplied no later than one month before the fair. The useful comparison is therefore not simply PLN 700 versus PLN 2,500. It is how much material the team can present clearly without paying for unused display space.</p>

<p>Start with the list, not the furniture. Group the titles that are genuinely ready for foreign-rights conversations. Remove books with unclear territorial rights, missing English-language information, or no one assigned to follow up after the fair. Then estimate whether one shelf can hold the working list or whether a rack would make the catalogue easier to navigate.</p>

<p>The rules require the quantity sent to fit the ordered space. If too much material arrives, the Institute may display only part of it or require the applicant to collect the excess at its own expense. The full service fee can still remain due.</p>

<h2 class="mt-10 mb-4 text-2xl font-bold text-gray-900">Know what the service includes</h2>

<p>The participation rules list more than display space. The service includes transport from an Institute-designated point in Poland to the fair and return transport to the applicant, subject to the delivery conditions. The applicant pays to get the material to the designated Polish collection point.</p>

<p>The package also includes one fair admission pass, one online catalogue entry, access to the collective stand's infrastructure, and shared reception service. For rack bookings, the Institute will produce and place the applicant's logo if a correctly prepared vector file is supplied. A poster can be produced instead of placing books on the rack when the applicant sends the required file.</p>

<p>These services can reduce the practical burden of a separate exhibition setup. They do not replace rights preparation, meeting outreach, staffing, or follow-up.</p>

<h2 class="mt-10 mb-4 text-2xl font-bold text-gray-900">Keep three access decisions separate</h2>

<p>The collective-stand application is one decision. General fair admission is another. The <a href="https://www.bolognachildrensbookfair.com/en/visit/general-information/2412.html">fair organizer</a> says Bologna Children's Book Fair will run <strong>April 5-8, 2027</strong> and is reserved for publishing-sector professionals. Its listed professional categories include publishers, agents, scouts, authors, illustrators, translators, licensors, and licensees.</p>

<p>Poland's Guest of Honour programme is a third surface. Bologna confirms that Poland will hold that role in 2027 and says the programme will include meetings, debates, and workshops, but the <a href="https://www.bolognachildrensbookfair.com/en/about/the-bcbf-guest-of-honour-countries/12900.html">detailed agenda is still forthcoming</a>. Do not treat a collective-stand application as registration for those sessions or as a promise of special access.</p>

<p>The clean sequence is:</p>

<ol class="mb-6 list-decimal pl-6 space-y-2">
  <li><strong>Confirm that the organization is eligible.</strong> Match its role and catalogue to the Institute's rules.</li>
  <li><strong>Register in the portal.</strong> The public call requires an account before the application can be submitted.</li>
  <li><strong>Choose the display size from a rights-ready title list.</strong> Do not use the full catalogue by default.</li>
  <li><strong>Read the Polish rules and portal instructions.</strong> Check file, delivery, payment, and display obligations before signing.</li>
  <li><strong>Submit by December 8.</strong> Keep the application separate from any later acceptance or allocation notice.</li>
  <li><strong>Plan fair admission and meetings separately.</strong> One included pass does not solve staffing or appointment logistics.</li>
</ol>
