<p>Frankfurter Buchmesse has opened its 2027 rights-centre booking cycle. The first decision is not how many meetings you hope to hold. It is whether your organization belongs in the Literary Agents &amp; Scouts Centre (LitAg) or the Publishers Rights Centre (PRC).</p>

<p>The distinction is firm. <a href="https://www.buchmesse.de/en/exhibit/table/litag">LitAg is reserved for independent literary and scouting agencies</a>. The <a href="https://www.buchmesse.de/en/exhibit/table/publishers-rights-centre">PRC is exclusively for publishers</a> and is built for their rights and licensing teams. If neither description fits, do not force the application. A general <a href="https://www.buchmesse.de/en/exhibit/table/workstation">workstation</a> or a trade-visitor plan may be the better route.</p>

<p>The early-bird discount ends <strong>November 30, 2026</strong>. That is a price cutoff, not the final registration deadline. Frankfurt's <a href="https://www.buchmesse.de/en/exhibit/information-exhibitors/book-fair-year">2027 exhibitor calendar</a> gives <strong>January 31, 2027</strong> as the official end of registration.</p>

<p>These prices exclude VAT. Frankfurt says the LitAg price includes obligatory fees and describes the PRC price as a package price that includes them. Optional shelving and graphic walls cost extra. Check the live product page and the order confirmation before budgeting.</p>

<h2 class="mt-10 mb-4 text-2xl font-bold text-gray-900">Choose by legal role, not by price</h2>

<p>LitAg costs much less, but it is not a lower-priced PRC. Its eligibility rule is the first gate. An independent literary or scouting agency can use the centre's appointment-controlled space, with a table, four chairs, lockable storage, power, Wi-Fi, a company sign, directory and calendar listings, and an agent pass.</p>

<p>A publisher should use the PRC route. Its package includes a table with four chairs, storage, power, Wi-Fi, a company sign, two exhibitor passes, 15 trade-visitor day tickets, directory and calendar listings, and support at the PRC information desk. The higher fee buys a different package for a different participant type.</p>

<p>Authors attending without an eligible agency or publisher should not assume either centre is a direct author-booking product. Start with the organization that will apply, identify its legal role, and then use the matching form.</p>

<h2 class="mt-10 mb-4 text-2xl font-bold text-gray-900">Calculate the useful cost, not just the discount</h2>

<p>The early-bird saving is <strong>EUR 44</strong> for one LitAg table and <strong>EUR 113</strong> for one PRC table, before VAT. Those are real savings, but neither is large enough to rescue the wrong booking.</p>

<p>Before applying, divide the full expected cost by the number of meetings that genuinely need a fixed rights-centre base. Include travel, accommodation, VAT treatment, optional fittings, and staffing. Then ask a simpler question: would those meetings work with ordinary fair access and pre-arranged space elsewhere?</p>

<p>A table is useful when the calendar is dense enough to benefit from one address and predictable visitor handling. It does not guarantee a particular location, meetings, counterparties, or deals.</p>

<h2 class="mt-10 mb-4 text-2xl font-bold text-gray-900">The 2027 schedule changes the weekend plan</h2>

<p>Frankfurter Buchmesse 2027 runs <strong>October 6 to 10</strong>, but the dedicated LitAg and PRC spaces will operate only from <strong>Tuesday, October 5 through Friday, October 8</strong>. The current <a href="https://www.buchmesse.de/files/media/pdf/terms-and-conditions-collective-stand-work-centre-workstation.pdf">participation terms</a> list 9:00 a.m. to 6:30 p.m. on those four days and require the tables to be cleared by the end of Friday.</p>

<p>That does not ban weekend rights meetings. Frankfurt says registered agents, scouts, and rights managers may meet in other business areas on the fairgrounds. Table holders who need weekend space should contact the relevant centre so its team can help find a location.</p>

<p>Build two calendars now: one for meetings that need the dedicated centre, and another for Saturday or Sunday conversations that can move elsewhere. Do not discover on Friday afternoon that the room attached to every weekend invitation is no longer available.</p>

<h2 class="mt-10 mb-4 text-2xl font-bold text-gray-900">An application is not an acceptance</h2>

<p>The official LitAg and PRC forms are live on Frankfurt's <a href="https://www.buchmesse.de/en/exhibit/registration">registration page</a>. They must be completed, signed, and returned through the route stated on the form.</p>

<p>The participation terms add a point worth reading before anyone signs: submitting the form is a binding application. The contract is completed when Frankfurt issues an order confirmation or first invoice. The organizer says there is no legal right to admission, and space allocation does not follow application order alone.</p>

<p>That means the November 30 price date should prompt a clean decision, not a rushed claim that a table has been secured.</p>

<h2 class="mt-10 mb-4 text-2xl font-bold text-gray-900">A five-step booking check</h2>

<ol class="mb-6 list-decimal pl-6 space-y-2">
  <li><strong>Confirm the applicant type.</strong> Independent literary or scouting agency means LitAg; publisher means PRC.</li>
  <li><strong>Count table-dependent meetings.</strong> Separate them from conversations that can happen elsewhere.</li>
  <li><strong>Budget the complete package.</strong> Use the before-VAT fee, passes included, travel, staffing, and any optional fittings.</li>
  <li><strong>Move weekend appointments.</strong> Contact the relevant centre for help with other business-area space.</li>
  <li><strong>Read the current form and terms before signing.</strong> Keep the confirmation and invoice separate from the initial application.</li>
</ol>
