<p>We’re in the final stages of preparing our next major release, a book by the renowned author Kornel Makuszyński, titled <b>Underdog Expedition</b>. We’re currently working on translating this beautiful, adventure-filled story about three boys and their dog.</p><p><b>Underdog Expedition </b>is a novel written by Kornel Makuszyński, originally published in 1936. The story follows a group of boys who embark on a journey full of challenges and humorous situations, often accompanied by their loyal dog. The novel is celebrated for its wit, lively characters, and its portrayal of friendship and bravery in the face of adversity. Makuszyński&#39;s works, including this one, are beloved in Polish literature for their ability to entertain while offering valuable moral lessons to young readers.</p>
