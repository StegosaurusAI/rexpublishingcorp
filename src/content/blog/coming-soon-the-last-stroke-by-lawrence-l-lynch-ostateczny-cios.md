<p>The Last Stroke by Lawrence L. Lynch is a classic of American detective fiction, set in a quiet lakeside town in the late 19th century. Detective Francis Ferrars is drawn into a murder investigation, with a delicate romance unfolding in the background, adding warmth and depth to the story. This is a novel full of twists and suspenseful atmosphere that every fan of detective literature should experience. Perfect for evening reading – it allows readers to dive into a world of mysteries and intrigue, from which it’s hard to pull away until the very last page.</p><p>Polish:</p><p>Ostateczny cios Lawrence L.
