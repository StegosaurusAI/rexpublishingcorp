Turin's Book to Book fellowship application and paid Rights Centre access are separate planning decisions. An application is not a selection result; a published table price is not a confirmed booking. Keep both statuses visible before committing a travel budget.

The organizer lists the [Rights Centre](https://www.salonelibro.it/rights-centre.html) for **May 12–14, 2027**, while the [public fair](https://www.salonelibro.it/eng.html) runs **May 13–17**. That difference matters when deciding which days your professional work requires.

## First check whether the application fits your role

The organizer-linked [2027 fellowship form](https://docs.google.com/forms/d/e/1FAIpQLScvjOR680ywqcl1YlDaD2guLUvK0YUmnigDBPfcVKY4CA7VwA/viewform) identifies **foreign publishers, editors, rights managers, literary agents and scouts** as its audience. It gives a submission deadline of **December 9, 2026, at 18:00 Italian time**.

The first visible step asks for company, role, language and profile information, plus previous fellowship selection. Prepare those details before starting. We inspected that step, not a completed application or subsequent screens; this is not an end-to-end application walkthrough.

Do not assume an independent author or translator qualifies solely because their work concerns books. Ask the organizer to clarify an uncertain role. Nor should a screen-rights professional treat this as a Book to Screen fellowship: the form specifically names **Book to Book**.

This guide establishes no travel support, accommodation, selection timetable or other fellowship benefit. Do not put assumed support into your budget. Applying and receiving a confirmed offer belong in different columns.

The equipment tables list one included ticket for option 1 and two for options 2 and 3; each lists appointment-platform access. Additional tickets are EUR 91 for six days, EUR 51 for the three centre days, or EUR 28 for one centre day, VAT included.

An additional ticket is not automatically a table or a platform account. Confirm the package for each attendee. These are published offers, not verified inventory or a tested purchase route; check current terms and equipment before choosing.

## Keep a decision record, not a presumed outcome

The following is **Rex editorial advice**, not an official application template. Give each unresolved question an owner and leave its status open until you have evidence.

Do not subtract an imagined fellowship award from the paid alternative. Keep the latter as a separate estimate until an actual offer tells you what changes. This avoids making a spending decision depend on an application whose outcome you do not yet know.

The Rights Centre page also displays a January 13 registration-opening notice without a year. We cannot establish a 2027 opening date from that wording and do not report general registration as currently open. Clarify the applicable route with the organizer before attempting a booking.
